Safety Tips

Stay safe online

Learn what to do and what to avoid when you receive suspicious messages or links.

💡

Scammers create a sense of urgency to trick you. Take a moment to verify before you trust.

  • Don't send money to anyone you don't know or trust
  • Don't share OTPs, passwords, PINs, or bank details
  • Don't click links in unexpected messages or emails
  • Don't download attachments from unknown senders
  • Don't respond to urgent threats about account blocking
  • Don't trust unexpected lottery or prize notifications
  • Don't pay registration fees for job or internship offers
  • Don't install apps from links sent in messages
  • Verify the organization through its official website
  • Contact companies using official phone numbers or emails
  • Report suspicious messages to cybercrime portals
  • Take time to think before making any payment
  • Use ScamShield AI to scan messages before acting
  • Check URLs carefully for misspellings or odd domains
  • Enable two-factor authentication on important accounts
  • Warn friends and family about scams you encounter
🎣 Phishing

Fake emails and messages that look like banks or companies trying to steal your login details.

💼 Fake Jobs

Scammers offer jobs or internships but ask for registration or training fees upfront.

🎁 Fake Rewards

"You won a prize!" messages that ask you to pay a fee or share personal information to claim it.

⏰ Urgency Tricks

"Act now or your account will be blocked" — scammers pressure you so you don't have time to verify.

How It Works

ScamShield AI uses a two-layer approach for reliable detection:

1
Local Detection

Rule-based analysis instantly checks for known scam patterns — money requests, urgency words, sensitive info requests, suspicious URLs, and more.

2
AI Analysis

When available, OpenAI-powered analysis provides deeper context and a human-readable explanation. Local and AI results are combined for the most accurate risk score.

ScamShield AI is a digital safety assistant, not a law enforcement or financial authority. Always verify important requests through official channels.
